Delivery & FAQ
Can flowers arrive today?

The public website states online orders before 11 a.m. are eligible for same-day delivery. Product lead time, capacity and destination still need confirmation. Sunday is closed.

What happens after the cutoff?

Online orders after 11 a.m. are processed the next business day according to the public homepage. Ask the store about other options; this concept cannot confirm them.

Can I choose an exact delivery time?

The merchant’s public policy does not guarantee exact times. No date or delivery-time promise is made by this demo.

Can I customize an arrangement?

The public catalog includes color, wrapping, cards and other custom options. Approved choices and prices vary by product and need client validation.

Is this a real store?

No. This is an independent concept by Phasefield. All cart actions are simulated. Product images are original illustrations, not merchant photography.
