What changes, and why?

Timing first

An urgent visitor needs cutoff and eligible timing before falling in love with a bouquet.

Room to explore

A discovery visitor can browse by style and budget. The same catalog stays underneath.

Consent first

Secret Admirer needs its process explained before a specific-date expectation forms.

The shopper stays in control.

An approved campaign label selects a prepared state. Visitor choice overrides it. Unknown context restores normal browsing. Prices do not change; no identity inference or live AI is used.

Test the business value.

Compare the improved default with adaptive treatment among equivalent campaign visitors. Measure revenue per qualified session, purchase conversion and operational guardrails. First validate the merchant’s data and policies.

The demonstration walkthrough

  1. Show the strong default and a reference product.
  2. Follow the urgent campaign link; choose a budget.
  3. Open presenter controls; test 11 a.m. and Sunday.
  4. Switch to discovery, then Secret Admirer.
  5. Simulate adaptive failure; show the default and retained demo bag.
  6. Explain the measured pilot, including the downside.

What this does not prove

No production Shopify installation, verified checkout, live inventory, actual customer demand or conversion lift. Original visuals are category illustrations. Browser/accessibility/performance QA must be read with its stated limits.

Business positioning: research, buying-mission design, custom implementation, measurement and learning using the simplest suitable commerce technology.
